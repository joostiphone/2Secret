# Chrome Web Store / Firefox Add-ons listing (English)

**Name:** 2Secret

**Summary (132 characters max):**
Free, open-source password manager: fill logins, one-time codes and passkeys from your end-to-end encrypted vault.

**Description:**

2Secret is a free, open-source password manager. Your vault is encrypted on
your own devices with your master password and a Secret Key; the server (ours
or your own) never sees a password, a website or even a title.

The extension puts your vault in the browser:

- Suggestions for the website you are on, and a one-click fill of user name,
  password and one-time code. Nothing is filled without your click, and only
  into pages that belong to the item's website.
- Passkeys: save them in your vault and sign in with them from any browser.
- Password generator, favorites, tags, search.
- With the 2Secret desktop app: an AI agent that works in your browser can
  ask 2Secret to sign you in. You allow it in the app, 2Secret fills the
  sign-in page, and the agent never sees the password.

Works with the 2Secret apps for macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad and
Android, and with the 2Secret command line. Sync through 2secret.app or your
own server; the code is on GitHub.
