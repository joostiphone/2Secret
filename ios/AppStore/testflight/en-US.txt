2Secret 1.14.1

WHAT'S NEW
- Face ID and Touch ID: the lock screen no longer says "not available right now" when it asks while the app is still coming to the front. It waits until the app is in front and asks again once; if the automatic prompt does not work, the button stays without an error.
- When Face ID or Touch ID really cannot be used, the message says why: locked after too many attempts, turned off or nothing enrolled, or fingerprints/face changed (then turn it on again in Settings).
- "Could not reach the server" now says why (name not found, connection refused, certificate not trusted).
- Still new since 1.14.0: vaults in iCloud Drive or a folder on your iPhone.

PLEASE TEST
1. Quit 2Secret completely and open it again, several times: Face ID or Touch ID should ask by itself, without a red message.
2. Switch to another app and back, and let the app lock; unlock with the Face ID / Touch ID button.
3. If you see a message about Face ID or Touch ID: take a screenshot and tell us when it appeared.
4. Folders (from 1.14.0): Settings → Vaults → New vault → A folder in a cloud service → iCloud Drive → Choose…; add an item, quit and reopen the app.
5. As always: AutoFill in Safari, syncing with your other devices.
