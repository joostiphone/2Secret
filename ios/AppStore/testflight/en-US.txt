2Secret 1.16.4

WHAT'S NEW
- A security update of a building block 2Secret uses for web addresses (url 2.5.9): a web address with thousands of nested "blob:" prefixes could crash a program that asked for its origin. 2Secret itself never asks for that, so you will not notice a difference.
- Everything else is as in 1.16.3: safer shared vaults, rounded cards on iPad in every orientation, a top bar that lines up next to scroll bars, and a link to r/2Secret.

PLEASE TEST
1. As in 1.16.3: in a shared vault, remove a member or use Replace vault key. Your other devices and the remaining members should take the new key without a message that a key was refused.
2. As always: syncing with your other devices, AutoFill in Safari with Face ID or Touch ID, and opening the website of an item.
