2Secret 1.14.2

WHAT'S NEW
- AutoFill in Safari and other apps no longer stops at "keychain error -25308". The 2Secret panel asked for Face ID or Touch ID before it was on screen, which iOS refuses. It now waits until the panel is shown, asks once more by itself if needed, and otherwise shows "Face ID or Touch ID could not be asked just now" with a Try again button.
- Still new since 1.14.1: Face ID and Touch ID in the app ask reliably when it opens. Since 1.14.0: vaults in iCloud Drive or in a folder on your iPhone.

PLEASE TEST
1. In Safari, open a site where you have a login (for example google.com), tap the username field and choose 2Secret above the keyboard or from the key icon. Face ID or Touch ID should ask, and the login should fill in.
2. Do this several times, also right after opening Safari and after unlocking the phone.
3. If a message appears in the 2Secret panel: take a screenshot, tap Try again, and tell us whether it then worked.
4. Fill a passkey and a verification code if you use them.
5. As always: opening the app with Face ID or Touch ID, syncing with your other devices.
