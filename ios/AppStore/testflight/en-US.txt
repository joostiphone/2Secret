2Secret 1.13.0

WHAT'S NEW
- Choose where each vault is kept: on the sync server, only on this device, or on a WebDAV server (Nextcloud, ownCloud, a Synology or QNAP NAS, Koofr…). Encrypted as always; only your devices can open it.
- Move a vault: long-press it (or Settings → Vaults) → Move…
- A vault you moved to a WebDAV server on another device appears here by itself. A vault moved to iCloud Drive or Dropbox on a Mac or PC shows where it is (folders on iPhone come later).
- Slide a finger along the tab bar to go from Home to Items to Watchtower.
- A livelier Watchtower ring, with a scale that shows where your score is.

PLEASE TEST
1. Slide along the tab bar, slowly and quickly; let go on each section. A plain tap should work as before.
2. Watchtower and Home: does the ring look right in light and dark mode?
3. If you have Nextcloud or a NAS: Settings → Vaults → New vault → A WebDAV server. Use "Test the connection" with a wrong and the right password, create the vault, add an item and see it arrive on another device.
4. Move a vault to "This device only" and back to the sync server; nothing should get lost.
5. Unlock with Face ID and with your master password; AutoFill in Safari as before.
