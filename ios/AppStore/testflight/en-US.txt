2Secret 1.11.0

WHAT'S NEW
Agent Access, part two, for the Mac, Windows and Linux apps with the browser extension: an AI agent that works in your browser (Claude for Chrome, Comet, Atlas, Playwright) can ask 2Secret to sign you in. You allow it in the app, 2Secret fills and submits the sign-in page, and the agent never sees the password. One button connects the extension to the app in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Arc and Firefox. The iPhone and iPad app has no new features in this version; it gets the same core library.

PLEASE TEST
1. Update and open the app: all your vaults and items are there. Unlock with Face ID and with your master password.
2. Sync (Settings → Sync now): it finishes without warnings.
3. AutoFill in Safari: sign in to a website with a saved login, and use a one-time code where you have one.
4. Share a vault with a second account and remove it again; share an item via a link and open the link in Safari.
5. Everything from 1.10 as before: the tab bar, long-press menus, selecting several items, search with favorites first, Watchtower.
