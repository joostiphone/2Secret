2Secret 1.9.2

WHAT'S NEW
- Your Secret Key is no longer included in iCloud and computer backups of your iPhone or iPad, so a backup never holds it next to your encrypted vault.
- AutoFill is stricter on websites where everyone gets a subdomain of their own (such as github.io): a login is only suggested on its own site.
- 2Secret connects to a sync server only over https://.
- Security fixes for syncing and shared vaults.

PLEASE TEST
1. Update over 1.9.1 and unlock: all your vaults and items are there, and syncing with your server works.
2. AutoFill: fill a password in Safari and in an app. Your logins still appear under "Suggested", also on subdomains (a login for google.com on accounts.google.com).
3. A shared vault: change an item and check that the change arrives on another device, and the other way round.
4. Copy a password and paste it somewhere. It is cleared after the time you set.
5. Unlock with Face ID, and fill a one-time code from AutoFill.
