2Secret 1.16.0

WHAT'S NEW
- A light sidebar: Settings → Appearance → Sidebar → Light. It applies with the light theme: a soft grey panel with the open item as a white card. With the dark theme the sidebar stays dark.
- On iPad in landscape and other wide screens, the sidebar is a card with rounded corners, and its items are softer.
- Importing a 1Password export (.1pux) refuses a file whose contents don't match its own index.
- New in the other apps of this version: unlock with fingerprint or face and AutoFill on Android, passkeys for Windows 11, and "Check for Updates…" in the desktop menu.

PLEASE TEST
1. Settings → Appearance → Sidebar: switch between Dark and Light, with the light and the dark theme. The sidebar and its open item should stay readable in every combination.
2. On iPad in landscape: the sidebar should be a rounded card with some space around it; in portrait and on iPhone nothing should change.
3. As always: AutoFill in Safari with Face ID or Touch ID, opening the app after it was in the background, syncing with your other devices, and shared-vault invitations.
