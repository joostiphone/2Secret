2Secret 1.11.1

WHAT'S NEW
The AutoFill panel in 2Secret's own look: when you fill a password, passkey or verification code in Safari or another app, the panel now shows the 2Secret logo and colours, follows the theme you chose in the app (light, dark or system), names the website it is for, and lists your logins with their website icons. The Face ID dialog says what happens ("2Secret is trying to unlock your vault for github.com"). Nothing changed in what AutoFill can fill.

PLEASE TEST
1. Update and open the app once (that refreshes what AutoFill may fill), then sign in to a website in Safari through the password suggestion and through "Passwords…" at the bottom of the list: the panel has the 2Secret logo and the website name at the top.
2. Switch the theme in 2Secret (Settings → Appearance) to dark and to light, open the app once, and fill again: the panel follows.
3. In the list, a login with a website icon in 2Secret shows that icon; one without shows the key symbol.
4. Fill a one-time code where you have one, and sign in with a passkey where you have one.
5. Everything else as in 1.11: unlock with Face ID and your master password, sync, sharing, Watchtower.
