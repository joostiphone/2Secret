2Secret 1.15.0

WHAT'S NEW
- A vault someone shares with you now arrives as an invitation on Home: you see the vault's name, who shares it and the fingerprint of their keys, and choose Accept or Decline. Nothing of the vault is shown or filled in before you accept. Accepting on one device is enough: your other devices add it by themselves.
- Settings → Devices: "Sign out all other devices".
- A new master password now also signs out AI agents elsewhere.
- Behind the scenes: the sync server is more strictly protected against misuse (storage limit, sign-in limits, size of one sync).

PLEASE TEST
1. Ask someone (or use a second account) to share a vault with you. Within a minute an invitation should appear on Home. Compare the fingerprint with the other person, then tap Accept: the vault and its items should appear.
2. On another device of yours: the vault should appear by itself, without a second invitation.
3. Have a second vault shared with you and tap Decline: it should disappear and not come back.
4. Settings → Devices → Show → Sign out all other devices: your other devices should ask you to sign in again.
5. As always: AutoFill in Safari with Face ID or Touch ID, opening the app, syncing with your other devices.
