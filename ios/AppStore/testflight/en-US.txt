2Secret 1.9.1

WHAT'S NEW
- Bigger tap targets: menu items, buttons and other controls are at least 44 points high on iPhone and iPad.
- Medium-width screens (iPad mini in portrait, iPad in Split View or Slide Over): the list and the item show side by side, and the menu opens next to the list.
- The app keeps clear of the status bar, camera and home indicator on every side, also in landscape.

PLEASE TEST
1. Tap through the menu, the list and an item (copy, show password, edit, favorite). Does every tap hit the control you meant, the first time?
2. Turn your iPhone to landscape, both ways. Nothing may sit under the camera, the rounded corners or the home indicator.
3. On iPad: try portrait, landscape, Split View at different sizes and Slide Over. Do the list and the item fit side by side, and do the menu and the back button work?
4. The basics still work: unlock with Face ID, fill a password with AutoFill in Safari, and sync with your server.
