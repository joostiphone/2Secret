2Secret 1.15.1

WHAT'S NEW
- Terms of Use. Using 2Secret means accepting the Terms of Use (2secret.app/terms, in English). The app says so under Create account and Sign in, and once on Home on devices that were already signed in.
- Settings → About now links to the Terms of Use and the privacy policy.

PLEASE TEST
1. Open the app on a device that was already signed in: Home should show a "Terms of Use" message once. "Read" should open the terms in the browser; after "OK" the message should not come back, also not after closing and opening the app.
2. Settings → About: "Terms of Use" and "Privacy policy" should each open the right page.
3. Signing in on a new device (or in a new profile): under the Sign in button it should say that signing in means agreeing to the Terms of Use, with a working link. Home should then not show the message.
4. As always: AutoFill in Safari with Face ID or Touch ID, opening the app, syncing with your other devices, and shared-vault invitations.
