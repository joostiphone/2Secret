2Secret 1.14.0

WHAT'S NEW
- Vaults in folders on iPhone and iPad: keep a vault in iCloud Drive or in a folder on your iPhone (also one that Möbius Sync keeps in sync). Encrypted as always; only your devices can open it.
- New vault (or Move…) → A folder in a cloud service → Choose… opens the Files app's folder picker.
- A vault you moved to iCloud Drive on your Mac shows "Choose the folder" here. Choose it once; vaults you move there later then appear by themselves.
- Settings → Vaults → Add a vault from a folder: open a vault another device keeps in a folder.
- Google Drive, OneDrive and (often) Dropbox don't let other apps choose a folder on iPhone; a direct connection to them comes later.

PLEASE TEST
1. Make a vault in iCloud Drive: Settings → Vaults → New vault → A folder in a cloud service → iCloud Drive → Choose… Add an item, quit and reopen the app: the vault and item should still be there.
2. Put the same vault on your Mac: in the Mac app, Settings → Vaults → Add a vault from a folder → choose iCloud Drive/2Secret. Change an item on one device and see it arrive on the other (iCloud can take a minute).
3. On the Mac, move a vault from the sync server to iCloud Drive. On the iPhone, open it and tap "Choose the folder"; then move a second vault there on the Mac: does it appear on the iPhone by itself?
4. Try a folder On My iPhone, and if you use it, a Möbius Sync folder.
5. Move a vault from a folder back to the sync server; nothing should get lost.
6. As before: unlock with Face ID, AutoFill in Safari, the tab bar.
