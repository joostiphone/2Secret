2Secret 1.16.3

WHAT'S NEW
- Shared vaults are safer: when a vault gets a new key (after you remove someone, or with Replace vault key), the new key proves that it was made by someone who had the old one. Your devices refuse a key without that proof, so the server cannot slip in a key of its own.
- On iPad in portrait, in Split View and in narrow windows, the sidebar, the item list and the top bar are rounded cards, as in landscape.
- On a Mac that always shows scroll bars, the top bar now lines up with the content below it; the page scrolls under the bar, which stays at the top.
- Settings → About links to the 2Secret community on Reddit (r/2Secret).

PLEASE TEST
1. In a vault you share with someone: remove a member, or use Replace vault key. Your other devices and the remaining members should get the new key and keep working, without a message that a key was refused.
2. Members who still have 2Secret 1.16.2 or older: a key they replace is refused by updated devices. Update everyone, then replace the key again.
3. iPad in portrait and in Split View: the list and the top bar should be rounded cards; in landscape it should look as in 1.16.2.
4. Mac with System Settings → Appearance → Show scroll bars → Always: on the dashboard, the top bar should line up exactly with the search field and the sections. Scroll down: the bar stays at the top and the page slides under it.
5. Settings → About: the r/2Secret link should open Reddit.
