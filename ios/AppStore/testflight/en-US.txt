2Secret 1.9.3

WHAT'S NEW
- A new look in the style of Liquid Glass (iOS 26): Dashboard, Items and Watchtower are in a floating tab bar of glass at the bottom, with a search button beside it. The content scrolls on under the tab bar, the status bar and the Dynamic Island. The status bar follows 2Secret's theme.
- A long press on an item gives a menu: copy, favorite, edit, rename, move, duplicate, share, select, trash. A long press on a vault in the menu too.
- Select several items at once (the check button above the list) to favorite, move or trash them together.
- Search shows your favorites first.
- In the menu, categories, vaults and tags fold away; tags start folded.
- Fetching website icons for all logins goes on when you leave Settings, shows how far it is, is much faster and finds many more icons.
- iPhone Duo: 2Secret fills the whole screen, open and closed. Built with Xcode 27.1 for iOS 27.

PLEASE TEST
1. Update and open the app: all your vaults and items are there. Unlock with Face ID.
2. The tab bar: tap Dashboard, Items and Watchtower. Scroll through a long list: the content runs on under the bar and the last item still comes above it.
3. Search: tap the search button at the bottom right; the list opens with the keyboard already up. Search for something that is also in your favorites: the favorite comes first.
4. At the top: scroll up; the content blurs under the buttons and the Dynamic Island. Choose light and dark under Settings → Appearance → Theme: the time and the battery stay readable.
5. Long-press an item and try its options, such as rename and copy password. Letting go does not also open the item or select any text. Scrolling opens no menu.
6. Long-press a vault in the menu at the top left: new item, edit, share.
7. Tap the check button, pick a few items and move them to the trash; restore them there.
8. Fold the tags in the menu open and closed; after restarting the app it is remembered.
9. Settings → Website icons → Fetch for all logins: go to another screen; fetching goes on and the icons appear.
10. iPhone Duo: use 2Secret closed and open; it fills the screen.
11. One-time codes, AutoFill in Safari and the iPad work as before.
