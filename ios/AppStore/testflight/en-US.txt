2Secret 1.15.2

WHAT'S NEW
- No more black or white screen after the app was in the background for a while. iOS sometimes ends the part of the app that draws the screen when it needs memory; until now the screen then stayed empty until you closed the app by hand. 2Secret now notices and starts again by itself, on the lock screen. Because iOS often tells the app only when you come back, it can close once as you open it: just tap it again.

PLEASE TEST
1. Open 2Secret and go to the Home Screen. Then open a few heavy apps (the camera, a game, Safari with many tabs) and use them for a few minutes. Go back to 2Secret: you should see the app, or it closes once and opens normally on the lock screen when you tap it again. You should no longer get a black or white screen.
2. Normal switching for a moment (copy a code, come back) should work as before, without a restart.
3. As always: AutoFill in Safari with Face ID or Touch ID, opening the app, syncing with your other devices, and shared-vault invitations.
