2Secret keeps your passwords, passkeys, cards, notes, identities and other secrets safe – end-to-end encrypted, open source, and under your control.

SECURITY FIRST
• Everything is encrypted on your device before it is stored or synced (XChaCha20-Poly1305, 256-bit keys).
• Your vault is protected by your master password and a 130-bit Secret Key that never leaves your devices. Even a stolen server database is useless without both.
• Sharing with family or colleagues uses quantum-resistant encryption (X25519 + ML-KEM-768).
• Unlock with Face ID or Touch ID. The master password is still asked every 14 days, so you never forget it.
• Fully open source: anyone can check how it works.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• Use 2Secret on this device only – no account with us, no server needed.
• Or sync between your devices through your own 2Secret server (a free Docker container on a Raspberry Pi, NAS or VPS). We never see or store your vault.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Logins, passkeys, credit cards, bank accounts, identities, passports, Wi-Fi, SSH keys, API keys, software licenses, secure notes and more.
