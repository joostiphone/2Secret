iPhone Duo: 2Secret now fills the whole screen, open and closed.

Security improvements:
- Your Secret Key is no longer included in iCloud and computer backups, so a backup never holds it next to your encrypted vault.
- AutoFill is stricter on websites where everyone gets a subdomain of their own: a login is only suggested on its own site.
- 2Secret connects to a sync server only over https.
- Safer syncing of shared vaults.
