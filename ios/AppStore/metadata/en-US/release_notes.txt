Importing from 1Password works again, safer shared vaults, and a tidier layout on iPad.

- Importing a 1Password export (.1pux) works again. 1.16.0 refused such files with "export.data missing from .1pux file".
- When a shared vault gets a new key, the key proves it was made by someone who had the old one; a key without that proof is refused. Update 2Secret on every member's device.
- On iPad, the item list and the top bar are rounded cards, in landscape and in portrait, and the top bar lines up with the content below it.
- Settings → About links to the 2Secret community on Reddit.
