Keep a vault in iCloud Drive or in a folder on your iPhone, next to the sync server and WebDAV. Always end-to-end encrypted: only your own devices can open it.

- New vault or Move → A folder in a cloud service → choose the folder in the Files app.
- A vault you moved to iCloud Drive on your Mac: choose the folder once, and vaults you move there later appear by themselves.
- Face ID and Touch ID ask reliably when the app opens, and say why when they cannot be used.
- "Could not reach the server" now says why.
