A new look: Dashboard, Items and Watchtower are in a floating tab bar of glass at the bottom of the screen, with a search button beside it. The content runs on under the Dynamic Island.

- Long-press an item or a vault for a menu of options, such as rename and copy password.
- Select several items at once to move them, favorite them or move them to the trash.
- Search shows your favorites first.
- Fold categories, vaults and tags in the menu away.
- Website icons are found faster and far more often.

iPhone Duo: 2Secret now fills the whole screen, open and closed.

AutoFill in 2Secret's own look: the panel for passwords, passkeys and verification codes shows the website it is for, your logins with their website icons, and follows the app's theme.

AI agents elsewhere: an AI agent on another computer (Claude Code in the cloud, a server) can ask for a password or code through your sync server, and you answer on your iPhone while 2Secret is open. The server cannot read the question or the answer. Pair agents in Settings → Agents elsewhere.

Security improvements:
- Your Secret Key is no longer included in iCloud and computer backups, so a backup never holds it next to your encrypted vault.
- AutoFill is stricter on websites where everyone gets a subdomain of their own: a login is only suggested on its own site.
- 2Secret connects to a sync server only over https.
- Safer syncing of shared vaults.
- 2Secret remembers the keys of the people you share vaults with and of your emergency contacts, and warns you when the server reports other keys for them.
- Share links: the server keeps only a hash of what opens a link.
