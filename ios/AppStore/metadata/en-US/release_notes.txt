Choose where each vault is kept: on the sync server, only on this device, or on a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, ownCloud or a Synology or QNAP NAS. Always end-to-end encrypted: only your own devices can open it.

- Move a vault from one place to another: long-press it, or Settings → Vaults → Move. Everything is copied and checked before the old place is cleared.
- A vault moved to a WebDAV server on another device appears on your iPhone by itself.
- Slide a finger along the tab bar to go from Home to Items to Watchtower.
- A livelier Watchtower ring, with a scale that shows where your security score is.
