Choose where each vault is kept, now also with iCloud Drive and your own server. Everything stays end-to-end encrypted: only your own devices can open your vaults.

New since version 1.12.1:

- Choose per vault where it is kept: on the sync server, only on this device, in iCloud Drive or a folder on your iPhone or iPad (also one that Möbius Sync keeps in sync), or on a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, ownCloud or a Synology or QNAP NAS.
- Move a vault at any time: long-press it, or Settings → Vaults → Move. Everything is copied and checked before the old place is cleared.
- Your other devices follow: a vault moved to a WebDAV server appears by itself. For a vault your Mac moved to iCloud Drive, choose the folder once; vaults moved there later appear by themselves.
- Add a vault that another device keeps in a folder or on a WebDAV server (Settings → Vaults).
- Slide a finger along the tab bar to go from Home to Items to Watchtower.
- A livelier Watchtower ring, with a scale that shows where your security score is.
- Face ID and Touch ID ask reliably when the app opens and when AutoFill fills a password, and say why when they cannot be used.
- AutoFill no longer stops at "keychain error -25308". If Face ID or Touch ID cannot be asked, tap Try again.
- A vault someone shares with you arrives as an invitation, with who shares it and their key fingerprint; nothing of it is used until you accept.
- "Could not reach the server" now says why.

The Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive apps do not let other apps choose a folder on iPhone and iPad; a direct connection to those services is planned.
