Thank you for reviewing 2Secret, a free, open-source password manager.

TRYING IT WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
No account with us is needed. On the first screen tap "Create a new account", enter any name, e-mail address and a master password (at least 12 characters), choose "Only on this device" and tap "Create account". The vault then stays on the device.

DEMO ACCOUNT (SYNC AND SHARING)
The demo account in the User name and Password fields lives on our sync server, sync.2secret.app, and already holds sample items and a shared vault. To sign in:
1. On the first screen tap "Sign in to an existing account".
2. Keep "2Secret server" selected.
3. E-mail address: the user name above.
4. Secret Key: [SECRET KEY OF THE DEMO ACCOUNT]
5. Master password: the password above.
The Secret Key is a second secret that 2Secret combines with the master password; it never leaves the user's devices, so every new device asks for it once.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings → Danger zone → Delete server account deletes the account and its data from the server.

AUTOFILL
Turn on "Unlock with Face ID" in the app under Settings → Security, then enable 2Secret under iOS Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords. AutoFill then offers logins, passkeys and verification codes in Safari and other apps.

OTHER NOTES
- No in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Vault contents are end-to-end encrypted on the device; the server cannot read them.
- Source code: https://github.com/joostiphone/2Secret
